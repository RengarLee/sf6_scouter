# 0.5.5 - 2026-11-15

### Added

- **Export your battle records**: In Settings → Data, export all local battle records for the signed-in account as CSV, regardless of pagination or filters. The file contains 12 columns covering time, mode, result, both players’ characters, scores and names, replay ID, and each player’s round results. Excel can read the Chinese text correctly.

### Improved

- **Release notes follow the app language**: View release notes in Chinese, English, or Japanese according to the current app language, without scrolling through the other languages.
